In a world increasingly saturated with high-fidelity graphics, immersive 3D environments,
and AI-generated "perfection," there is a growing counter-movement. We crave the raw, the
unfiltered, and the functional. This is not just nostalgia for the early web; it is a
rejection of the dopamine-loop design patterns that have come to dominate our screens.

### The Return of the Terminal

Why are developers obsessing over terminal-based portfolios? It represents a return to
control. The command line is honest. It does exactly what you tell it to do, nothing more,
nothing less. It hides nothing behind a glossy gradient.

When I set out to build this OS-style portfolio, the goal wasn't just to mimic macOS. It
was to strip it back. To remove the blur (mostly), the bounce, and the shine, and replace
it with mono-spaced typography and clear, stark borders.

### Function over Form? No, Function IS Form.

The "Nothing" aesthetic — popularised by the tech brand — suggests that the transparent
workings of a device are beautiful in themselves. We shouldn't hide the grid. We shouldn't
hide the pixels. We should celebrate the machine.
